## Boundaries

- No new backend, payment, real-member, AI or social feature.
- No cloud identity claim: runtime remains OFFLINE_DEMO. Keep a short truthful state where needed.
- No image replacements, route reshuffling or unrelated feature changes.
- Keep disclosure controls on the card fields; simplify customer navigation rather than remove privacy enforcement.
